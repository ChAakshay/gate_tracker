## 🧠 Why This System Eliminates Cognitive Overload

Rigid schedules fail because of the **"What-The-Hell Effect"**: if you wake up late or your workout runs long and you miss the *08:00 AM* slot, your brain subconsciously says *"today is already ruined"* and you abandon the day.

In this **Target-Based System**:
* You have **complete autonomy over WHEN you study**. Morning, afternoon, or midnight—it doesn't matter.
* You only focus on **1 Daily Card** at a time.
* If life interrupts you, you don't recalculate calendars. You simply execute the card when you're free.
