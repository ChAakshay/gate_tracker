### 🧬 The 5 Topper Principles That Actually Matter

These are not generic advice — these are patterns extracted from AIR <100 toppers across multiple years:

#### Principle 1: "PYQs Are the Exam"
> *"I solved 20 years of PYQs 4 times. By the third round, I could predict question patterns."* — AIR 12, GATE 2024

GATE recycles concepts, not questions. 60–70% of any year's paper tests the same conceptual buckets as previous years. PYQs are not practice — they ARE your primary textbook.

#### Principle 2: "The Error Log Changed Everything"
> Every wrong answer gets dissected: What concept? Why wrong? What's the fix? This log is reviewed every 3 days. By exam day, you've systematically eliminated every category of mistake you're prone to making.

#### Principle 3: "Foundation Subjects Are Force Multipliers"
> Engineering Math + Signals & Systems + Network Theory = the DNA of ECE. Master these first and every other subject becomes 40% easier because they all build on these foundations.

#### Principle 4: "Mock Analysis > Mock Score"
> Spending 2 hours analyzing a mock test is more valuable than spending 3 hours taking the next one. Toppers treat mocks as diagnostic tools, not scoreboards.

#### Principle 5: "Negative Marking Is the Silent Killer"
> The difference between a 55/100 and a 65/100 is often not what you got right — it's what you didn't get wrong. Discipline on MCQ attempts (only when ≥70% confident) is a 5–8 mark swing.

### 🏁 Exam Day Protocol

**The Night Before:**
- ✅ Bag packed: admit card, ID, pen, water bottle, transparent pouch
- ✅ Light revision of formula sheets only (30 min max)
- ✅ No new problems. No social media doom-scrolling about "expected cutoffs"
- ✅ Sleep by 10 PM

**Exam Morning:**
- ✅ Wake up 3 hours before exam
- ✅ Light breakfast (avoid heavy/oily food)
- ✅ 10-minute formula sheet glance — then STOP studying
- ✅ Arrive 45 min early. Settle in. Deep breaths.

**During the Exam (3 hours = 180 minutes):**

| Phase | Time | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Scan** | 0–5 min | Skim entire paper. Mark easy/medium/hard mentally. |
| **Harvest** | 5–60 min | Attempt ALL easy questions first (GA + obvious ones). Target: 35–40 marks locked. |
| **Build** | 60–140 min | Tackle medium-difficulty questions. Use full working. Target: 25–30 more marks. |
| **Snipe** | 140–170 min | Attempt hard questions ONLY if confident ≥70%. Prioritize NAT/MSQ (no penalty). |
| **Review** | 170–180 min | Check flagged answers. Verify units and signs on NATs. Do NOT change gut answers on MCQs. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The #1 exam-day rule**: If you're stuck on a question for >4 minutes, FLAG IT and move on. Come back later. The marks you lose by wasting time on one hard question could have been earned on three easy ones.
