## 🟢 STAGE 1: The Unshakable Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

### 🔹 WEEK 1: Linear Algebra & Verbal Aptitude
* **Target Contribution**: **8 Marks**.
* **Day-by-Day Syllabus Matrix**:
  * **Mon**: Matrix Algebra, Properties of Determinants, Matrix Inversion, Row Echelon reduction & Rank calculation.
  * **Tue**: Systems of Linear Equations ($Ax=b, Ax=0$), Consistency testing ($\text{Rank}(A)$ vs $\text{Rank}([A|b])$), Unique/Infinite/No-solution conditions.
  * **Wed**: Characteristic Equation, Eigenvalue properties ($\sum \lambda_i = \text{Tr}(A), \prod \lambda_i = \det(A)$), Eigenvectors & Linear Independence.
  * **Thu**: Cayley-Hamilton Theorem ($A^{-1}$ & $A^k$ matrix polynomials), Matrix Diagonalization ($A = PDP^{-1}$), Quadratic Forms & Sylvester’s criterion.
  * **Fri**: Vector Spaces, Subspaces, Basis, Dimension, **Rank-Nullity Theorem** ($\text{Rank} + \text{Nullity} = n$). GA: Subject-Verb Agreement, Tenses, Conjunctions.
* **Tier 1 (80/20 Golden Topics)**: Eigenvalues/Eigenvectors, Cayley-Hamilton, Rank-Nullity Theorem, Consistency of $Ax=b$.
* **Topper Trap to Avoid**: Confusing the geometric multiplicity (number of independent eigenvectors) with algebraic multiplicity (root multiplicity of $\lambda$). Non-diagonalizable matrices have geometric < algebraic multiplicity.
* **Exit Criteria**: 85+ PYQs solved, 4 A4 Short Notes pages, $\ge 80\%$ on Saturday Subject Test 1.

* **Target Contribution**: **+7 Marks (15 Marks Locked)**.

  * **Thu**: Vector Calculus: Gradient ($\nabla \phi$), Directional Derivative along unit vector $\hat{u}$, Divergence ($\nabla \cdot \vec{V}$), Curl ($\nabla \times \vec{V}$), Solenoidal and Irrotational fields.
  * **Fri**: Vector Integral Theorems: Line integrals, Green’s Theorem in plane, Stokes’ Theorem, Gauss Divergence Theorem. GA: Percentages, Ratios, Speed-Distance, Work-Time.
* **Tier 1 (80/20 Golden Topics)**: Maxima/Minima ($rt-s^2$), Leibniz Rule, Directional Derivative, Gauss Divergence Theorem.
* **Topper Trap to Avoid**: Forgetting to convert vectors to **unit vectors** when calculating directional derivatives ($\nabla \phi \cdot \frac{\vec{a}}{|\vec{a}|}$).

Steady-State Error ($e_{ss}$) and Error Constants ($K_p, K_v, K_a$) for Type 0, 1, 2 systems.

### 🔹 WEEK 16: Random Processes, Noise & Transmission Lines (EMTL)
* **Target Contribution**: **+3 Marks (71 Marks Locked)**.
* **Day-by-Day Syllabus Matrix**:
  * **Mon**: Random Processes: Strict-Sense Stationary (SSS) vs **Wide-Sense Stationary (WSS)** processes, Mean function, **Autocorrelation Function** $R_{XX}(\tau) = E[X(t)X(t+\tau)]$ properties ($R_{XX}(0) = \text{Total Power}$, $R_{XX}(\tau) = R_{XX}(-\tau)$, $|R_{XX}(\tau)| \le R_{XX}(0)$).
  * **Tue**: **Power Spectral Density (PSD)**: $S_{XX}(f) = \mathcal{F}\{R_{XX}(\tau)\}$ (Wiener-Khinchin Theorem), Cross-Correlation & Cross-PSD, Transmission of WSS process through LTI system ($S_{YY}(f) = S_{XX}(f)|H(f)|^2$, $R_{YY}(\tau) = R_{XX}(\tau) * h(\tau) * h(-\tau)$).
  * **Wed**: Noise in Systems: Thermal (Johnson) Noise ($S_N(f) = \frac{N_0}{2}$ AWGN), Noise Equivalent Bandwidth, Noise Figure ($F = \frac{\text{SNR}_{in}}{\text{SNR}_{out}}$), Friis Formula for cascaded stages ($F_{total} = F_1 + \frac{F_2-1}{G_1} + \frac{F_3-1}{G_1 G_2}$).
  * **Thu**: **Transmission Line Theory (70% of EMTL)**: Distributed parameter model ($R, L, G, C$), Characteristic Impedance $Z_0 = \sqrt{\frac{R+j\omega L}{G+j\omega C}} \approx \sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}$ for lossless, Propagation Constant $\gamma = \alpha + j\beta$.
  * **Fri**: Lossless Line Impedance & Matching: Reflection Coefficient $\Gamma = \frac{Z_L - Z_0}{Z_L + Z_0}$, **Voltage Standing Wave Ratio** ($\text{VSWR} = \frac{1+|\Gamma|}{1-|\Gamma|}$), Input Impedance Formula $Z_{in}(l) = Z_0 \frac{Z_L + j Z_0 \tan\beta l}{Z_0 + j Z_L \tan\beta l}$, Quarter-Wave Transformer ($Z_{in} = \frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}$ at $l = \lambda/4$), Half-Wave Line ($Z_{in} = Z_L$ at $l = \lambda/2$).
* **Tier 1 (80/20 Golden Topics)**: WSS Noise through LTI Filters ($S_{YY} = S_{XX}|H|^2$), Transmission Line $Z_{in}$ at $\lambda/4$, VSWR and $\Gamma$ calculations, Friis Cascaded Noise Formula.
* **Topper Trap to Avoid**: Mixing up $\Gamma$ at the load vs $\Gamma$ at the input. $|\Gamma|$ remains constant along a lossless line, but its phase rotates by $-2\beta l$.
* **Exit Criteria**: 95+ PYQs, 4 A4 Short Notes pages, $\ge 80\%$ on Saturday Subject Test 12.

### 🔹 WEEK 17: Analog/Pulse Modulation & Uniform Plane Waves (EMTL)
