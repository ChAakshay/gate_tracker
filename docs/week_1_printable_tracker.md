### 🚨 Emergency Protocols (When things break)
- **Overslept < 1 hr**: Cut breaks to 5 min, skip GA theory.
- **Overslept 1–2 hrs**: Skip Spaced Revision (4 PM). Shift Active Recall to 5:40 PM.
- **Lost 3+ hrs**: Execute **Minimum Viable Day** (Theory Block 1 + Math PYQ Block 1 + Short Notes + Error Log = 3.5 hrs).
- **🔴 Red Energy Day**: Sleep until awake. Do 1 hr PYQs + 30 min Error Log review + 15 min Short Notes. Total 1.75 hrs. No guilt.

---

### 🚦 The 5 Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **NEVER start a block asking "What do I do now?"** (Desk prepped at 9:10 PM night before).
2. **NEVER spend >6 minutes on a single PYQ** (Use 3-Pass Rule SOP 3).
3. **NEVER skip the 8:30 PM Error Log** (Quality over sheer volume).
4. **NEVER skip Sunday rest** (Offline downtime consolidates memories).
5. **NEVER study new theory after 4 PM** (Afternoon/Evening is for practice & recall).
