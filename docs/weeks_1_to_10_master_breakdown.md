* **Theme**: Foundation of all matrix operations & core verbal grammar.
* **Subjects**: Engg. Mathematics (Linear Algebra) + General Aptitude (Verbal).

* **Friday**: Vector Spaces, Subspaces, Linear Independence, Basis & Dimension, **Rank-Nullity Theorem** ($\text{Rank} + \text{Nullity} = n$). GA Verbal Review.

## 🔹 WEEK 6: Fourier Transforms & P-N Junction Diode
* **Theme**: Frequency domain signal representations & junction diode physics.
* **Subjects**: Signals & Systems (Fourier Series & CTFT) + Electronic Devices (P-N Diode).
* **Cumulative Marks Target**: **38 Marks**.

### Day-by-Day Targets (Mon–Fri)
* **Monday**: Continuous-Time Fourier Series (CTFS): Trigonometric & Exponential Fourier series, Dirichlet conditions, Symmetry properties (Even, Odd, Half-wave), Parseval's Power Theorem.
* **Tuesday**: Continuous-Time Fourier Transform (CTFT): Definition, Standard transform pairs ($\text{rect} \leftrightarrow \text{sinc}$, $\delta \leftrightarrow 1$, $e^{-at}u(t)$, $e^{j\omega_0 t}$).
* **Wednesday**: CTFT Properties: Linearity, Time shifting, Frequency shifting (Modulation), Time scaling, Duality theorem, Frequency differentiation/integration, Parseval's Energy Theorem ($E = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int |X(\omega)|^2 d\omega$).
* **Thursday**: P-N Junction Physics: Built-in potential $V_{bi} = \frac{kT}{q}\ln\frac{N_A N_D}{n_i^2}$, Depletion region width $W = \sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon}{q}(\frac{1}{N_A}+\frac{1}{N_D})V_{bi}}$, Electric field profile.
* **Friday**: Diode I-V characteristics: Shockley ideal diode equation, Temperature dependence of reverse saturation current, Junction capacitances (Depletion capacitance $C_j \propto 1/\sqrt{V_R}$ vs Diffusion capacitance $C_d \propto I_F$), Zener & Avalanche breakdown.
