### Day 1: Matrices, Determinants, Rank & Systems of Equations

#### 1. Morning Block (Subject A — Engg. Math | 4 Hours)
* **Topics to Cover**:
  * Matrix properties (Symmetric, Skew-Symmetric, Hermitian, Skew-Hermitian, Orthogonal, Unitary).
  * Determinants (properties, fast computation tricks).
  * Rank of a Matrix (Echelon form, Row reduction, linearly independent rows/columns).
  * Systems of Linear Equations ($Ax = b$ and $Ax = 0$): Consistency, Unique Solution, Infinitely Many Solutions, No Solution conditions using $\text{Rank}(A)$ and $\text{Rank}([A|b])$.
* **Recommended Resource**:
  * **Book**: B.S. Grewal — Chapter 2 (Linear Algebra)
  * **Video**: NPTEL Dr. S.K. Gupta (IIT Roorkee) — Lectures 1 to 4 *OR* GateWallah Linear Algebra Playlist (Speed 1.25x).

#### 2. Afternoon Block (Subject B — GA Verbal | 2 Hours)
* **Topics to Cover**:
  * Core English Grammar rules for GATE: Subject-Verb Agreement, Tenses, Modifiers, Parallelism.
  * GATE error patterns in sentence correction.
* **Recommended Resource**:
  * CareerRide / GateWallah Verbal Ability basics (YouTube) or IndiaBix Grammar Rules.

#### 3. Evening Practice & Recall Block (3 Hours)
* **PYQ Target (2 Hours)**: Solve **20 GATE PYQs** specifically on Matrix Rank & Linear Systems ($Ax=b$).
* **Active Recall & Notes (1 Hour)**:
  * Close all books and answer these on paper:
    1. *What is the rank condition for $Ax = b$ to have a unique solution? Infinitely many? No solution?*
    2. *What is the determinant of an Orthogonal matrix? What about a Skew-Symmetric matrix of odd order?*
  * Create **Short Notes Page 1** (only key formulas, properties, and conditions).

### Day 2: Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & Properties

#### 1. Morning Block (Subject A — Engg. Math | 4 Hours)
* **Topics to Cover**:
  * Characteristic equation $|A - \lambda I| = 0$.
  * Properties of Eigenvalues:
    * $\sum \lambda_i = \text{Trace}(A)$
    * $\prod \lambda_i = \det(A)$
  * Eigenvalues of special matrices (Symmetric, Skew-Symmetric, Orthogonal, Triangular, Diagonal).
  * Eigenvectors and their linear independence.
* **Recommended Resource**:
  * **Book**: B.S. Grewal — Section 2.14–2.17
  * **Video**: NPTEL Dr. S.K. Gupta — Lectures on Eigenvalues/Eigenvectors.

#### 2. Afternoon Block (Subject B — GA Verbal | 2 Hours)
* **Topics to Cover**:
  * Vocabulary building strategy for GATE (High-frequency root words, prefixes/suffixes).
  * Sentence Completion (Fill in the blanks using contextual clues).

#### 3. Evening Practice & Recall Block (3 Hours)
* **PYQ Target (2 Hours)**: Solve **20 GATE PYQs** on Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors.
* **Active Recall & Notes (1 Hour)**:
  * Answer without looking:
    1. *If $A$ has eigenvalues $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$, what are the eigenvalues of $A^k$, $A^{-1}$, $A^T$, and $A + cI$?*
    2. *What are the eigenvalues of a Hermitian matrix vs. Skew-Hermitian matrix?*
  * Add properties to **Short Notes Page 2**. Log any calculation errors in your **Error Log**.

### Day 3: Cayley-Hamilton Theorem, Matrix Powers & Quadratic Forms

#### 1. Morning Block (Subject A — Engg. Math | 4 Hours)
* **Topics to Cover**:
  * Cayley-Hamilton Theorem ($A$ satisfies its own characteristic equation).
  * Expressing higher powers $A^n$ and $A^{-1}$ using Cayley-Hamilton.
  * Diagonalization of matrices ($A = PDP^{-1}$).
  * Quadratic Forms: Matrix representation, Definiteness (Positive Definite, Positive Semi-Definite, Negative Definite, Indefinite) using eigenvalues and principal minors (Sylvester's Criterion).
* **Recommended Resource**:
  * **Book**: B.S. Grewal — Section 2.18–2.20
  * **Video**: NPTEL / GateWallah Cayley-Hamilton & Quadratic Forms.

#### 2. Afternoon Block (Subject B — GA Verbal | 2 Hours)
* **Topics to Cover**:
  * Word Analogies (identifying relationships: synonyms, cause-effect, degree, part-to-whole).
  * Solving previous 10 years of GATE GA Analogy questions.

#### 3. Evening Practice & Recall Block (3 Hours)
* **PYQ Target (2 Hours)**: Solve **20 GATE PYQs** on Cayley-Hamilton and Quadratic Forms.
* **Active Recall & Notes (1 Hour)**:
  * Answer without looking:
    1. *How do you test if a $3 \times 3$ symmetric matrix is Positive Definite using principal minors?*
    2. *If $A^2 - 5A + 6I = 0$, what is $A^{-1}$ in terms of $A$?*
  * Add to **Short Notes Page 3**.

### Day 4: Vector Spaces, Basis, Dimension & Rank-Nullity Theorem

#### 1. Morning Block (Subject A — Engg. Math | 4 Hours)
* **Topics to Cover**:
  * Vector spaces, Subspaces (testing subspace criteria).
  * Linear Independence and Dependence of vectors.
  * Basis and Dimension of a vector space.
  * Null space, Column space, Row space.
  * **Rank-Nullity Theorem**: $\text{Rank}(A) + \text{Nullity}(A) = n$ (where $n$ is the number of columns).
* **Recommended Resource**:
  * **Book**: Kreyszig or B.S. Grewal (Vector Spaces section)
  * **Video**: Gilbert Strang (MIT 18.06 - Lectures 6, 7, 14) *or* GateWallah Linear Algebra.

#### 2. Afternoon Block (Subject B — GA Verbal | 2 Hours)
* **Topics to Cover**:
  * Critical Reasoning & Logical Deduction (Inferences, Assumptions, Strengthening/Weakening arguments).
  * GATE Reading Comprehension techniques (skimming, main idea identification).

#### 3. Evening Practice & Recall Block (3 Hours)
* **PYQ Target (2 Hours)**: Solve **15 GATE PYQs** on Vector Spaces, Nullity, and Rank-Nullity theorem.
* **Active Recall & Notes (1 Hour)**:
  * Answer without looking:
    1. *State the Rank-Nullity Theorem and explain what nullity represents geometrically.*
    2. *If $A$ is a $4 \times 6$ matrix of rank 3, what is the dimension of its null space?*
  * Complete **Short Notes Page 4**.
